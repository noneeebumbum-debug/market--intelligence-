About the intelligence engine

This version combines explainable technical and quantitative rules in a central weighted engine. It is not a trained machine-learning model and its score is not a calibrated probability. The app has no account access and cannot place orders. Some advanced modules—historical walk-forward backtesting, trained ML, liquidation heatmaps and full historical volume profile—remain future development. It is not safe to assume any signal is profitable without testing.
